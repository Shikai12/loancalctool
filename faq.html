Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data really private?
Yes. All calculations happen entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your loan amounts, interest rates, or personal information. Your data never leaves your device.
Are these calculators accurate?
Our calculators use standard financial formulas. However, every lender has different fee structures, rounding rules, and terms. Always verify important numbers with your lender before signing anything. The calculators are for estimation and education, not contractual accuracy.
Do I need to sign up or create an account?
No. Every calculator on this site is free to use without registration, email, or account creation. We do not want your data — we just want you to understand your loans better.
Do you sell loans or recommend lenders?
No. We do not sell loans, collect leads, or take referral fees from banks or lenders. We may include affiliate links to financial products we believe are genuinely useful, but these do not influence our content or calculator accuracy.
Who is James Whitmore?
James is an independent financial consultant based in Denver, Colorado. He spent 14 years inside a bank's loan department before quitting to help people avoid the traps he used to sell. He has processed roughly 2,400 loan applications and knows which banks pad fees.
Can I share my calculator results?
Yes. Most calculators have an export or share feature. You can download your results as a PDF or share a link. Remember that shared links do not contain your personal data — they only store the calculation parameters.
What browsers are supported?
Our calculators work on all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. For the best experience, keep your browser updated. Internet Explorer is not supported.
How do I contact you?
Email us at privacy@loancalctool.org. We read every email, but replies may take a week or two. We do not offer professional consultation or financial advice via email.
