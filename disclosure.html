Disclosure

Transparency is how I built trust after 14 years inside a bank. Here is everything you need to know about how this site makes money.

Affiliate Links

Some pages on Loan Calc Tool contain affiliate links. If you click on an affiliate link and make a purchase or sign up for a service, we may receive a small commission at no additional cost to you. This helps support the site and allows us to continue providing free calculators and content.

We only recommend products or services we believe may be genuinely useful. We do not let affiliate relationships influence our content, reviews, or calculator accuracy.

Advertising

This site displays advertisements from third-party networks. These ads help cover the costs of hosting, development, and content creation. We do not control the specific ads shown, but we review ad networks to ensure they meet basic quality standards.

No Paid Reviews or Sponsored Content

We do not publish paid reviews, sponsored articles, or native advertising disguised as content. All articles are written by James Whitmore based on his independent research and experience.

No Referral Fees from Banks or Lenders

We do not have direct referral agreements with banks, credit unions, or online lenders. We do not collect leads or sell your contact information to financial institutions.

Questions?

If you have questions about our disclosure practices, contact us at privacy@loancalctool.org.
