How It Works

Add each of your current debts with balance and APR. Then enter the consolidation loan offer. The calculator simulates your current minimum-payment strategy versus the consolidation loan, showing total interest, payoff time, and net savings.

Practical tip: Consolidation only works if you stop adding to the original debts. Close or freeze the old cards after consolidation. Also watch out for origination fees on the new loan — they can eat into your savings.

Privacy note: All calculations happen in your browser. Nothing is sent to our servers.
