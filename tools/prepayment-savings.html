How It Works

Enter your current balance, rate, and remaining term. Use the slider to adjust your extra monthly payment. The calculator shows interest saved, months shaved off, and — crucially — warns you if your prepayment penalty would exceed those savings.

Practical tip: Before making extra payments, call your lender and ask: "Is there a prepayment penalty?" If yes, get the exact terms and enter them here. Some penalties only apply for the first 12-24 months. Timing matters.

Privacy note: All calculations happen in your browser. Nothing is sent to our servers.
