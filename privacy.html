Privacy Policy

✓ We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data. All calculations happen in your browser.

What We Do Not Collect

Loan Calc Tool does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. When you use our calculators, all data entered stays in your browser. We cannot see your loan amounts, interest rates, credit scores, or any other information you input.

Cookies and Analytics

We do not use tracking cookies. We may use basic, anonymized analytics to understand which calculators are most popular, but this data cannot identify you personally.

Third-Party Services

This site may display advertisements from third-party networks. These advertisers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve relevant ads. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Affiliate Links

Some pages contain affiliate links. Clicking these links may allow the third-party site to track that you came from Loan Calc Tool. We do not receive your personal data from these interactions.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at privacy@loancalctool.org.
